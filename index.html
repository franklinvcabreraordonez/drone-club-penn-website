Who We Are

The Drone Club @ Penn is a student organization exploring drone technology and its business applications.

Membership is open to all Penn students, regardless of major or school, and no drone experience is required. We hold general meetings weekly and occasionally host events that may take additional time. There is no fixed time commitment.

What We Do

The club covers three sides of the drone industry.

Hardware Engineering

  • Design airframes and components in CAD.
  • Build a functional prototype drone as a team.
  • Continuously test parts and improve designs.

Software Development

  • Program flight control and autonomy code.
  • Simulate code before applying it to hardware.
  • Analyze and test sensor and flight data for improved performance.

Business Strategy

  • Research how drones are used in industries like agriculture, delivery, inspection and defense.
  • Evaluate markets, costs and the regulations around commercial drone use.
  • Pitch a business case to the club.
